For each leader

Personally relevant.

A useful introduction, peer conversation or expert session can help a leader test an assumption, compare options and identify a next step. The support is shaped around their role and priorities.

For your company

Better-informed execution.

People across your leadership team make choices about customers, delivery, hiring and investment. Membership gives participating leaders a way to draw on outside experience while your team owns the decisions and execution.

Before your company decides

Put the practical questions on the table.

Use the fit conversation to discuss the people you have in mind and what would make participation useful. These are questions to resolve together, not terms to assume.

  • Who is eligible, and can a leader participate if their CEO does not?
  • Which formats are available, and what involvement and time-zone fit should members expect?
  • What does each membership include, what does it cost, and how does each person’s trial work?
  • How are sensitive discussions handled, including colleagues or competitors in the community?
  • What, if anything, is shared with a company sponsor, and how could we assess usefulness?
