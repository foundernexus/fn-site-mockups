Our story

Experience is better
when it gets passed around.

The next decision may be new to you. Someone else has a useful chapter to share.

Built on FounderNexus

Started by people who have built companies.

FounderNexus grew from a simple conviction: relevant experience should be easier to reach. Court Lorenzini, founding CEO of DocuSign, helped bring that conviction to life.

VEN Global builds on those roots, extending the focus to leadership teams of venture-backed companies. Founders, CEOs and senior leaders across functions are making the calls that turn capital into progress. Useful experience should reach each of them.

Our mission

Help leadership teams make better-informed decisions and execute their company’s plan.

We start with the work in front of each member. Each month, your VEN Partner focuses on your top two challenges and helps find a useful introduction, peer conversation or expert session. You bring that perspective back to the choices you own.

What makes a useful conversation

Enough in common to understand. Different enough to help.

Similar stages and responsibilities make the constraints familiar. Different experiences help you see another option, question an assumption or avoid a detour someone else has already taken.

Bring an honest question. Share what you have learned. Leave with something useful to take back to work.

Good company. For the company you are building.
