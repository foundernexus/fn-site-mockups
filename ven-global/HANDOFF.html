Upload and share

  1. Unzip VEN-Global-Team-Review.zip.
  2. Upload all extracted files and the assets folder together into your chosen static site's publishing folder. Keep their names and relative folders intact. index.html must be at that folder's top level.
  3. Open the published address to check the homepage, then share that address with your team. Share HANDOFF.html at the same address if you want reviewers to see this guide first.

No installation, build step, server-side application or API keys are required. The package works at a site's root or within a subfolder. If your host accepts ZIP uploads, upload the ZIP; otherwise upload its extracted contents. Pasting the HTML into a CMS editor is not equivalent to hosting these files.

What to evaluate

  1. Audience: Does a venture-backed leader recognize that the offer is for them and their leadership colleagues?
  2. Value: Is the monthly focus on each member's top two challenges, with a dedicated Partner and relevant peer experience, concrete and useful?
  3. Trust and tone: Does it feel credible, approachable and appropriate for consequential operating decisions?
  4. Next step: Is the path to exploring individual or team membership clear?

For feedback, record the page, section, what felt unclear and your suggested change. Distinguish an unclear promise from a visual preference.

Review boundaries

  • Forms are demonstrations. They do not send inquiries or save a membership application.
  • External links are real. Member login opens the existing FounderNexus portal. Blog articles and event destinations leave the mockup.
  • Events are a dated snapshot. The page contains October 2026 events collected during this review, not an automatically updated feed.
  • The Success Equation is illustrative. Its assumptions are not measured membership effects or forecasts of company success.
  • VEN is a proposed brand direction. Existing community references retain FounderNexus attribution. Operational terms and the production application integration still need confirmation before launch.
