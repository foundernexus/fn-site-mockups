Venture Executive Network · 6 October 2026 · v12.11

Personal support, clearer in practice.

VEN helps leaders at venture-backed companies work through the decisions behind their company’s goals. A dedicated Partner gets close to each member’s priorities, brings relevant expertise, resources and people into the work, and follows through as needs change.

Open the new versionOpen conventional site

Independent copy and design reviewers approved this version for local feedback. Copy clarity is approximately 9/10 as an editorial judgment. That is not a measured visitor-comprehension or conversion score.

What changed

  • A broader Partner story. The hero and “How VEN works” now explain understanding the work, finding the right support and staying alongside the member. Introductions are one way to help.
  • Less repetition between pages. The homepage keeps the engagement selector. Membership explains what working together involves, without repeating the same gallery.
  • Eight distinct role examples. CEO, sales, marketing, finance, product and technology, operations, people, and other responsibilities each show a relevant challenge, possible support and a decision to take back.
  • Credibility with context. Each role’s advisor profile has one relevant connection to the example. The repeated generic support paragraph and duplicate topic labels are gone.
  • Consistent next steps. Individual and company inquiry paths carry the same broader support story. Each eligible newcomer’s own trial and the monthly top-two focus remain clear.

Home gains 30 words to explain the wider offer; membership loses 43. The aim is clearer purpose and less duplicated explanation, with the existing section rhythm intact.

The five visitor questions

QuestionHow this version answers it
Is this for me?Venture-backed leadership teams in the hero; individual responsibilities and meaningful examples on the role pages.
Do I qualify?$1M+ in company funding, worldwide participation, founders, CEOs and senior leaders. Stage bands remain context, with no four-person limit.
Is it safe?Confidentiality is explicit. “What’s said here stays here.” The initial inquiry asks visitors to leave sensitive details out.
Do you understand my work?Specific trade-offs across functions, top-two monthly priorities and a Partner who learns the member’s constraints.
Can you provide value?Existing advisor biographies and member experiences remain. Support examples show what useful input can help someone decide, without invented results or guaranteed advisor access.

New non-CEO outcome stories will develop with the expanded offering. They are future proof collection, not a condition for sharing this version.

The hospitality design language

Calm, capable and personal. Spacious photo-led composition; readable Jakarta sans type; ivory, paper and mist surfaces; restrained steel-blue controls and copper details; soft corners and fine rules. Warmth comes from attentive language and relevant human imagery, with clear paths into the offer.

All 37 existing assets and all 10 original style files are preserved. One small additional style treatment supports the distinct role examples and shorter membership explanation. This is a focused refinement of v12.10.

Sharing the demo

For a normal multi-page demo, upload the contents of site/ to the web root. Its index.html is the homepage. The single HTML version can also be uploaded alone and opened using its filename. Photos and fonts are embedded; existing video, event, blog and member-login destinations still use their existing external links.

For useful feedback, ask: “After the first screen, what do you think VEN does and who is it for? Which part feels relevant to your role? What makes you trust it, and what is still unclear?”
