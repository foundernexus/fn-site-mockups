What the model calculates

The chance that every modeled decision goes to plan.

Baseline for everyone1.80%
One participating leader2.16%

Real decisions vary in importance and can be revisited. A company does not need every decision to go to plan to make progress.

See the calculation

Let n be the team size, d decisions per leader, m participating leaders, a the baseline probability, and b the support probability. Percentages are converted to decimals.

  • Total decisions: n × d
  • Baseline: a ^ (n × d)
  • One participating leader: b ^ d × a ^ ((n − 1) × d)
  • Selected participation: b ^ (m × d) × a ^ ((n − m) × d)

Each decision is treated as independent. The assumed support effect may be positive, zero, or negative. The comparison ratio is undefined if the baseline result is zero.
