# Main website v19: warmth and story revision

This is a new full-site mockup, separate from the v18 homepage explorations. The previous main mockup remains preserved in `ven-global-v14` and the existing v17.3 handoff.

## Audience and offer that must remain

- Leadership teams of venture-backed companies, including eligible leaders beyond founders, CEOs and the C-suite. No fixed four-person roster.
- Peer membership, with a dedicated VEN Partner focused on each member's top two challenges every month.
- Support shaped around role, stage and priorities, connecting relevant peers, operators, experts and sessions. Members own decisions and execution.
- Every new eligible leader has an individual trial, including the next colleague to join. No invented duration, price or operational terms.
- All four existing annual-revenue bands, funding labels and the contextual caveat.
- Relevant experience can inform decisions, reduce avoidable setbacks and support execution. No quantified membership effect, fabricated outcomes or company-success forecast.
- Connected team-decision example with named dependencies and a feedback relationship; it is illustrative, inclusive and owned by the company.
- Existing navigation: Membership, For your team, Success Equation, Our story, Events, Blog, Member login, and inquiry.
- Supplied VEN | global logo, local Plus Jakarta Sans, core navy and blue, actual supplied photography and historical testimonial/logo attribution.
- Clearly marked unsupplied member and company-sponsor evidence. Make the placeholders subordinate to the site's story.
- Calculator arithmetic, assumptions and JavaScript unchanged.

## Design and narrative acceptance

- The first screen identifies the audience, practical help and a clear invitation alongside real people.
- Warmth comes from recognition, reassurance, candid photography and conversational structure. Light visual personality must express the work, not disguise an institutional essay.
- Meaningful asymmetry and varied composition give the page character without turning every section into a card, ledger or diagram.
- Large, bounded photographs preserve faces and gestures. No sprawling empty-table imagery or clipped people.
- Seven concise homepage reading beats at most. Optional challenge exploration and detailed evidence remain off the homepage.
- Story begins with people and the reason for the community, then connects those roots to today's leadership-team offer.
- Team page explains how individual support can contribute to a shared plan, then makes participation approachable and practical.
- Local groups read as groups; section gaps allow breathing room. Important body copy remains at least 16px and captions at least 14px.
- Desktop, tablet and narrow phone layouts must avoid overflow, colliding controls, hidden essential copy and unjustified oversized imagery.
- Keyboard controls, readable contrast, current navigation and a useful script-unavailable layout remain.

## Review gate

The audit must identify the regression before implementation. Copy and design agents address it in the isolated source folder. An independent reviewer checks actual rendered home, Story and Team pages plus the shared journey, and rechecks any requested repairs. Completion requires an explicit final approval for mockup review, not merely technical checks or an invented conversion rating.
