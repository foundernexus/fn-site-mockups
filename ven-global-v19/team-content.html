The practical questions, before you decide

Put the practical questions on the table.

Tell us about the people you have in mind and what would make participation useful. These are questions to resolve together, not terms to assume.

  • Who is eligible, and can a leader participate if their CEO does not?
  • Which formats are available, and what involvement and time-zone fit should members expect?
  • What does each membership include, what does it cost, and how does each person’s trial work?
  • How are sensitive discussions handled, including colleagues or competitors in the community?
  • What, if anything, is shared with a company sponsor, and how could we assess usefulness?
