# FounderNexus messaging and visual refinement

September 25, 2026. Review of the working Map C homepage, compared with the September 21 messaging review and the current conversation. This is design judgment, not evidence of conversion performance.

## Main finding

The page was identifying the venture-backed audience without sufficiently acknowledging its operating pressure. The outcome headline works only when the surrounding copy explains the expectations, time constraints and consequential decisions facing that audience. The latest pass restores that connection without adding a new section.

## Messaging check

| Requirement | Assessment and action |
| --- | --- |
| Venture-backed pressure | Restored high expectations, limited runway and unfamiliar decisions in the hero. Growth targets and board expectations now frame the decision examples. |
| Leadership-team audience | Explicit in the hero, role examples and fit section. Individual leaders receive support relevant to their responsibilities; this is not a founder-only proposition. |
| Concrete delivery | Nexus Partner connects priorities to relevant experience. Monthly conversations and the three-step sequence explain the recurring relationship. |
| Operational benefit | Examples lead to a hypothesis to test, a clearer hiring scope, fundraising preparation or explicit product tradeoffs. The headline is an intended benefit, not a measured or guaranteed outcome. |
| Human connection | Early member attribution, a real session photograph and an unchanged quote give the proposition a human basis. Members also contribute their own experience. |
| Credibility | Founder and session-contributor roles are distinguished. The wider founding story and approved portraits would deepen this evidence. |
| Conversion path | Consistent Apply for membership wording, fit conversation and each eligible leader's individual trial. The application destination remains a prototype dialog. |

## Implemented in this pass

- Retained the two-line outcome headline. Added a 30-word supporting paragraph connecting venture pressure to Nexus Partner support, with the pressure sentence in darker text.
- Reinforced the pressure in the introduction to the existing practical examples.
- Linked the Nexus Partner steps with a fine vertical line and numbered circles. This makes the progression visible and echoes the connections in the hero illustration.
- Opened the credentials section into white editorial columns with larger names and restrained rules. This breaks the repeated pale-blue card treatment while retaining generous spacing.
- Corrected the missing visible space in the desktop application button.

## Highest-value opportunities next

1. **Build a recognizable connection motif.** Carry the same line-and-node language through the hero, Nexus Partner sequence and one member story. Use it to explain a relationship, keeping the official logo untouched. The first small application is now in the sequence.
2. **Feature one verified decision story.** Show the original challenge, the relevant conversation and the action the member took. A specific decision changed is stronger evidence than another broad benefit paragraph. Obtain the member's approved account before writing it as fact.
3. **Art-direct authentic people imagery.** Add approved portraits beside the founder/contributor credentials, with consistent crops and concise roles. Improve future session photography through closer framing on conversation and expression. Retain the real existing photos until those assets are available.
4. **Replace company text with approved marks when available.** Normalize their optical size and retain accurate relationship labels. Do not label testimonial companies as partners or imply company-wide endorsement.
5. **Refine the hero illustration's narrative.** The globe currently suggests reach, while the business value is relevant experience around a decision. A future version should keep the challenge visible, reveal the relevant connection, then show an illustrative next step. Use restrained motion, a pause control and a complete static reduced-motion state.

Preserve the official logo, Plus Jakarta Sans, #0072BA blue, navy hierarchy, readable copy and open spacing. Use selective emphasis and distinct section compositions to create richness. More badges, gradients and animation would compete with the people and proposition.

## Basis and validation

The messaging baseline is `FounderNexus-Homepage-Messaging-Review-2026-09-21.md`: venture expectations, decisions across the leadership team, relevant experience, Nexus Partner support and practical execution value. Commercial terms and delivery commitments need operating-team verification before launch.

The emphasis on explicit purpose, visitor value and a small number of prominent actions is consistent with [NN/g's homepage usability guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/113-design-guidelines-homepage-usability/). That guidance supports clarity; the specific visual recommendations above are design judgments for FounderNexus.

Desktop and narrow-phone visual checks cover the changed hero, connected steps and credentials treatment. Original embedded assets and both selected testimonials remain unchanged. The build, source-script syntax, section count and nonempty link checks pass. The existing export-runtime limitation and unfinished application destinations remain documented in EDITORIAL-AUDIT.md. No live/default-branch publication is included.
