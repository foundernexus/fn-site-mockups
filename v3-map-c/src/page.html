How membership works

Someone in your corner.
Focused on your priorities.

Your Nexus Partner is your point of contact. A focused monthly conversation connects your priorities to people and working sessions that can help you act.

  1. 01

    Make the priority clear.

    Bring the question, constraint or decision you are facing. Work with your Nexus Partner to identify where another perspective would help.

  2. 02

    Connect with the right experience.

    That may mean a peer introduction, an expert conversation or a focused group session. The format follows the challenge.

  3. 03

    Turn the conversation into a next step.

    Use what you learn to clarify your next move. In your next conversation, revisit what has changed and where you need help now.

Inside a working session +

Work through a specific topic with peers and an experienced session leader. These examples show the scope of the conversations:

  • Building Your AI for GTM: From Experiments to Workflows That Actually Run
  • How to Time and Structure a Seed Raise That Closes
  • Pitch deck review with a Bay Area VC

Illustrative session topics. This is not a live event calendar.

Put the experience to work

Different responsibilities.
Real decisions to make.

Use another leader’s experience to test your assumptions, weigh the tradeoffs and decide your next move.

Revenue & growth

“Why isn’t our sales motion repeatable yet?”

Compare your customer profile, sales process and conversion bottlenecks with a founder or revenue leader who has built that motion.

Leave with a sharper hypothesis to test.

People & operations

“Is this the right time for a senior hire?”

Pressure-test the scope, timing and expectations of the role with someone who has built that function at a similar stage.

Clarify the job before you fill it.

Finance & capital

“Are we ready for the next raise?”

Examine the story, milestones and operating plan with people who understand the process and the questions investors will ask.

Identify what needs work before outreach.

Product & technology

“What belongs on the roadmap, and what can wait?”

Compare customer demand, technical constraints and team capacity with a product or engineering leader who has faced similar tradeoffs.

Make the priorities and tradeoffs explicit.

Illustrative situations and intended takeaways. Each member’s experience will depend on their priorities.
