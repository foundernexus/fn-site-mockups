The Nexus Partner

Your advocate inside FounderNexus.

Your Nexus Partner is your point of contact inside FounderNexus. A focused monthly conversation clarifies your priorities and helps connect you with relevant peers, operators, experts and working sessions. As your priorities change, so does the support.

1

Clarify the pressing issues

A focused 1:1 surfaces the challenge, constraint, or inflection point you're carrying.

2

Identify the type of support you need

Some issues require a referral, an introduction, a 1:1 discussion or a group session. Your Nexus Partner helps identify the support most relevant to your situation.

3

Connect the next useful step

Your Nexus Partner helps you reach relevant people and resources while the decision is still live.
